Improve Delivery Efficiency and Reduce Operating Costs

Thematic Area: Smart Logistics and Asset Management

How might we identify and improve the delivery activities that create the greatest avoidable costs while maintaining reliable service?

Background

Delivery operations can incur avoidable costs through inefficient routing, service coverage and resource coordination. This challenge seeks technology partners that can identify practical ways to improve operating efficiency.

The objective is to select a measurable operating problem, test a suitable solution and measure its impact on performance. Applications to assess include route planning, service coverage and hub placement.

Define the workflow, operating constraints and success measures during project scoping. Relevant startup capabilities include logistics software, geospatial analysis, routing and operational analytics.

Significance of the Problem

  • Higher delivery costs: Avoidable travel, waiting time and poor coordination increase operating expenses.
  • Limited productivity: Inefficient operations constrain the number of deliveries completed with existing resources.
  • Slower expansion: Unclear operating bottlenecks complicate growth and service reliability.

Potential Market Size

  • The global route optimisation software market is estimated at US$12.2 billion in 2026, rising to US$21.5 billion by 2030. This indicates commercial demand for routing technology across transport applications, including delivery operations.

Qualification & Fitness

  • NUS founders and programme support: Have an NUS student or graduate founder, or have received incubation support through an NUS programme such as B71, PIER71, TIG or the National GRIP Programme.
  • NUS researchers and academics: NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs) and professors.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to startups from Indonesia and other countries.
  • BLOCK71 network: Startups from BLOCK71 centres in Singapore, the United States, Japan, China, Vietnam and Indonesia.
  • Commitment: Be ready to complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia
  • Timeline: November 2026–April 2027.
