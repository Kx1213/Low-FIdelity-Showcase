Improve Delivery Efficiency and Reduce Operating Costs

Thematic Area: Logistic & Shipping

How might we identify and improve the delivery activities that create the greatest avoidable costs while maintaining reliable service?

Background

Delivery operations can incur avoidable costs through inefficient routing, service coverage and resource coordination. This challenge seeks technology partners that can identify practical ways to improve operating efficiency.

The objective is to select a measurable operating problem, test a suitable solution and measure its impact on performance. Applications to assess include route planning, service coverage and hub placement.

Define the workflow, operating constraints and success measures during project scoping. Relevant startup capabilities include logistics software, geospatial analysis, routing and operational analytics.

Significance of the Problem

  • Higher delivery costs: Avoidable travel, waiting time and poor coordination increase operating expenses.
  • Limited productivity: Inefficient operations constrain the number of deliveries completed with existing resources.
  • Slower expansion: Unclear operating bottlenecks complicate growth and service reliability.

Potential Market Size

  • Global courier, express and parcel market: Mordor Intelligence estimates the market at US$725 billion in 2026, reaching US$963.9 billion by 2032.
  • Route-optimisation software market: Grand View Research estimates this global market at US$12.2 billion in 2026, growing to US$21.5 billion by 2030, covering software and related services across transport industries.
  • Indonesian delivery market: Mordor Intelligence estimates Indonesia’s courier, express and parcel market at US$8.42 billion in 2026, reaching US$11.91 billion by 2031. These figures measure delivery services.

Market Validation

Improve Delivery Efficiency and Reduce Operating Costs

  • Proven results in Indonesia: In a customer testimonial published by Locus, Tigaraksa Satria reported 10% better delivery-cost efficiency at its largest distribution centre after six months, while maintaining service levels. The company then began expanding the system to two more centres, showing that measurable savings support wider adoption. (Source: Locus (opens in a new tab))
  • Faster planning at higher volumes: Onfleet reports that Bayshore HealthCare cut route-planning time by up to 83% while scaling from 300 to 2,000 daily orders, without increasing its dispatch team in proportion to the extra volume. The case demonstrates the value of replacing manual routing as delivery demand grows. (Source: Onfleet (opens in a new tab))
  • Operators are investing in efficiency: J&T added 134 sets of automated sorting equipment in 2025, taking its global total to 413. Its Southeast Asian business also reduced cost per parcel to US$0.48, alongside strong volume growth. These operating results show that efficiency remains a commercial priority for large delivery networks. (Source: J&T Express (opens in a new tab))
