Bring Delivery Robots and Smart Services into Residential Communities

Thematic Area: Property

Problem Statement: How might we introduce delivery robots and other smart services into BSD City to make everyday life more convenient, improve the residential experience and attract more people to live, work and spend time there?

Background

Modern residential developments need more than housing and basic facilities to attract residents. Convenient services, new technology and engaging experiences can help make a township a more attractive place to live.

BSD City aims to strengthen its appeal as a place where people can live, learn, work and play. One opportunity is to introduce autonomous robots that can support everyday activities, such as delivering food, parcels and other items within residential communities.

The goal is to explore practical robotics solutions that improve convenience and create a more distinctive residential experience.

The objective is to identify robotics partners that can demonstrate suitable technologies, assess their use in residential environments and explore potential pilot opportunities in BSD City.

Relevant startup capabilities include autonomous delivery robots, robotic navigation, obstacle detection, fleet management and smart-city service integration.

Significance of the Problem

  • Limited residential differentiation: Without distinctive services and experiences, residential developments may struggle to stand out from competing townships.
  • Less convenient daily services: Residents may have fewer options for convenient deliveries and automated services within their communities.
  • Missed technology opportunities: Delayed adoption of robotics may limit opportunities to introduce new residential services and improve the township experience.
  • Challenges in robotics deployment: Robots must navigate safely around pedestrians, vehicles and buildings while operating reliably in real residential environments.

Potential Market Size

  • Autonomous Delivery & Service Robotics (Indonesia): Indonesia's Delivery Robots Market is projected to grow from USD 4.7 Million in 2025 to USD 21.1 Million by 2030 (34.9% CAGR). The broader Indonesia Robotics Market is estimated at USD 23.80 Million in 2025, reaching USD 47.75 Million by 2030 (14.94% CAGR). Opportunities cover Upstream (robot design, navigation and route planning), Midstream (testing, integration and residential pilot deployments), and Downstream (autonomous deliveries, robot fleet operations and resident-facing services).
  • Smart-City Technologies & Residential Services (Indonesia): The Indonesia Smart Cities Market is valued at USD 780 Million in 2025, projected to reach USD 2.18 Billion by 2032 (15.84% CAGR). Growth creates opportunities for robotics, connected infrastructure and automated services within residential townships, including smart mobility and technology-enabled community experiences.
  • Complementary factors: Rising demand for convenient delivery services; increasing adoption of smart-city technologies; growth in e-commerce and connected urban communities; and opportunities for property developers to differentiate residential developments through innovative services.

Qualification & Fitness

  • Technology Readiness: Startups with functional prototypes, pilot-ready or commercially available solutions in autonomous delivery, service robotics, smart navigation and residential technology.
  • NUS Linkage: Open to NUS students, graduates, researchers, principal investigators (PIs), professors and startups supported through NUS Enterprise programmes, including BLOCK71, PIER71, TIG and National GRIP. Local and international startups with relevant solutions are also welcome, including those across BLOCK71's global network.
  • Commitment to Follow Through: Selected startups should be prepared to participate in technical discussions, explore potential residential applications and travel to Indonesia for further engagement or pilot activities where required.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia.
  • Timeline: November 2026 – April 2027.
