Prepare Complete Solar Surveys and Customer Proposals

Thematic Area: Digital Operations and Automation

How might we connect site surveys, engineering inputs and commercial approvals to prepare clear solar proposals for commercial and industrial customers?

Background

An Indonesian solar EPC business serves commercial and industrial customers. This project focuses on structured surveys and proposal coordination to support its sales process.

The objective is to map incomplete information, repeated revisions, approvals and customer follow-up, then select a measurable process improvement. Relevant startups offer digital field surveys, quotation workflows and customer enquiry management.

The solution must help teams capture required information once, preserve approved assumptions and coordinate the handover between commercial and engineering staff. Retain specialist inputs and review for solar design and customer economics.

Significance of Problem

  • Slower proposal preparation: Gaps in surveys or approvals extend the work needed to assemble a complete customer offer.
  • Repeated information requests: Missing site records lead to additional field visits or exchanges between sales and engineering.
  • Unclear customer communication: Unassigned questions and inconsistent proposal revisions make purchasing decisions harder for customers.

Existing Efforts

  • Implementation priorities: Map current sales stages and tools, establish turnaround measures and select the sales-conversion improvement to address before procurement.
  • Available partner capabilities: Digital survey forms can capture locations, photographs and equipment details, including offline collection. B2B sales tools support quotations and approvals, while enquiry platforms assign customer questions and track their resolution.
  • Proposed collaboration: Select one actual bottleneck, such as survey completeness or quotation approval. Test a checklist or approved proposal template on a live opportunity. Engineers remain responsible for system design, load assessment, connection considerations, generation estimates and financial assumptions. Measure useful improvement against the current process.

Potential Market Size

  • Indonesian rooftop sector: Indonesia reported 916.99 MW of installed rooftop solar in April 2026. This provides local sector context for survey and sales-engineering tools; select the commercially addressable customer segment using project-fit criteria.

Eligibility / Qualification

  • Technology readiness: Minimum TRL3; suitability for a pilot assessed during selection.
  • NUS linkage: NUS student or graduate founder, or incubation through an NUS programme such as BLOCK71.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to Indonesian and overseas startups.
  • Commitment: Complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Where & When

  • Where: Project location set during pilot planning.
  • When: Schedule set during partner selection.
