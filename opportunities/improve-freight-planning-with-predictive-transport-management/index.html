Improve Freight Planning with Predictive Transport Management

Thematic Area: Logistic & Shipping

How might we use an AI-powered transport-management system with route prediction to improve freight planning and delivery performance?

Background

Improving freight transport planning requires a transport-management system (TMS) that combines operational records with predictive route analysis to support planning decisions.

The objective is to organise transport work, anticipate route conditions and improve the use of vehicles and delivery resources. Relevant startup capabilities include transport-management software, logistics optimisation, mapping and location analytics.

Assess the solution against the business’s existing systems, shipment requirements and operating constraints.

Significance of the Problem

  • Higher transport costs: Inefficient routes and resource allocation increase travel and operating expenses.
  • Less reliable delivery plans: Limited insight into route performance makes schedules harder to maintain.
  • Slower planning: Teams spend additional time comparing routes, assigning resources and responding to disruptions.

Potential Market Size

  • Global transport-management market: Grand View Research estimates the transportation management system market at US$18.6 billion in 2025, growing to US$68.4 billion by 2033. This includes systems that plan, coordinate and manage freight movements.
  • Route-optimisation software market: Grand View Research estimates this global market at US$12.2 billion in 2026, growing to US$21.5 billion by 2030, covering software and related services across transport industries.
  • Indonesian road-freight industry: Mordor Intelligence estimates road-freight services at US$57.5 billion in 2026, reaching US$75.95 billion by 2031. This represents the transport activity that planning solutions support, rather than spending on software.
