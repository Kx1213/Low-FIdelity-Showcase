Fraud & Theft Mitigation: Inline Quality Verification & Product Integrity

Thematic Area: Product Integrity & Quality Assurance

How might we continuously verify fluid quality and detect product adulteration during custody transfer operations through inline sensing technologies to secure supply chain integrity and eliminate off-spec crude deliveries across remote transfer points?

Background

Improvement is needed to address undetected product adulteration, fluid dilution, and quality degradation occurring during crude oil transport and custody transfer, which cannot be caught by static volume checks or periodic lab testing alone.

The goal is to deploy continuous inline quality sensing across active transfer lines, storage discharges, and transport offloading points. The objective is to establish real-time visibility over crude oil specification shifts, prevent diluted or adulterated fuel batches from reaching trading partners, and reconcile quality data at critical custody transfer points.

The work involves quality assurance and compliance leads, transport managers, site operations personnel and custody transfer auditors at remote storage facilities, pipeline manifolds, truck and barge loading arms, and transfer points. Monitoring runs during active pumping, batch transfers and custody handovers.

The approach equips transfer points with continuous inline fluid quality sensors capable of dynamic property analysis under high-flow conditions. This combines commercial custody transfer and quality compliance requirements with deep-tech ecosystem startups and university spin-offs specializing in inline fluid analytics and spectroscopy.

Significance of the Problem

  • Contractual & Financial Penalties: Severe monetary losses and rejected shipments when off-spec or adulterated crude oil reaches buyers or refineries.
  • Downstream Equipment Damage: Contaminated or chemically masked crude causing severe corrosion and catalyst poisoning in surface processing and refinery infrastructure.
  • Reputational Damage: Loss of trust and commercial standing with trading partners due to unverified product quality during custody handovers.
  • Dynamic Fluid Constraints: Dynamic fluid properties are difficult to measure continuously without causing flow restriction or pressure drops in high-volume transfer lines.
  • Post-Event Blindspots: Traditional laboratory sampling is slow and post-event, meaning adulterated product is often already mixed into storage tanks before test results arrive.

Potential Market Size

  • Market size and growth: The global fuel quality testing, oil analysis, and machine condition monitoring market is valued between USD 4.5 Billion and USD 6.2 Billion, driven by strict refining specs, custody transfer compliance, and international trade standards. Furthermore, the global energy supply chain loses over USD 133 Billion annually to illicit fuel trade, chemical adulteration, and inventory shrinkage.
  • Supporting factors: Mandatory compliance with international custody transfer specifications, strict corporate audit mandates for product purity, contractual zero-defect requirements.

Qualification & Fitness

  • Technology Readiness: Ready commercial and pilot-proven technology (TRL 7–8) requiring field validation on specific crude oil grades and transfer flow rates.
  • NUS Linkage: Built by research teams at the National University of Singapore (NUS) or facilitated through BLOCK71 centres (Singapore, United States, Japan, China, Vietnam, Indonesia), the Technology Innovation Programme (TIG), or the National GRIP Programme. Open to NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs), professors, as well as global and local technology providers specializing in inline fluid property sensing, spectroscopy, or continuous quality verification.
  • Commitment to Follow Through: Selected teams must participate in technical scoping calls to establish transfer line specifications and fly to Indonesia to instrument pilot transfer points.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Jakarta, Special Capital Region of Jakarta, Java (Scoping Calls) & Active Custody Transfer Points / Storage Depots, Indonesia.
  • Timeline: November 2026 – March 2027
