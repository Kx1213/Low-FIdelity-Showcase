Attract More Homebuyers and Improve Residential Property Sales

Thematic Area: Property

Problem Statement: How might we use better buyer insights, targeted marketing and engaging property experiences to attract potential homebuyers, improve customer engagement and turn more enquiries into residential property sales in BSD City?

Background

Residential property developers are increasingly exploring new ways to reach potential homebuyers, communicate the value of their developments and create more engaging property-buying experiences.

BSD City is looking for innovative ways to strengthen residential demand and attract more people to its developments. This includes improving how properties are promoted, how potential buyers experience the township and how sales teams engage with interested customers.

The goal is to make residential properties more attractive to potential homebuyers and improve the overall property-buying experience.

The objective is to identify technology and marketing solutions that help property developers understand buyer preferences, reach suitable customers and improve the conversion of enquiries into property purchases.

Relevant startup capabilities include property technology (PropTech), customer analytics, digital marketing, virtual property tours, interactive property showcases and customer relationship management.

Significance of the Problem

  • Opportunities for residential sales growth: Reaching suitable homebuyers and creating engaging property experiences can help developers attract stronger interest and improve sales conversion.
  • Lost sales opportunities: Broad marketing campaigns may reach people who are unlikely to purchase, increasing marketing expenses without generating suitable enquiries.
  • Lost sales opportunities: Limited customer engagement and inconsistent follow-up may cause interested buyers to lose interest before making a purchase.
  • Limited property differentiation: Without engaging experiences and clear value propositions, potential buyers may find it difficult to distinguish one residential development from another.

Potential Market Size

  • Residential Property Development & Sales (Indonesia): Indonesia's Residential Real Estate Market is valued at USD 47.99 Billion in 2026, projected to reach USD 58.70 Billion by 2031 (4.12% CAGR). Opportunities cover Upstream (buyer research, housing demand analysis and customer targeting), Midstream (property marketing, digital listings, virtual tours and buyer engagement), and Downstream (sales follow-up, customer relationship management and conversion of enquiries into purchases).
  • Property Technology & Digital Buyer Engagement (Asia Pacific): The Asia-Pacific PropTech Market generated USD 5.53 Billion in 2025 and is projected to reach USD 15.55 Billion by 2033 (14.0% CAGR during 2026–2033). Opportunities include digital property discovery, customer analytics, interactive property experiences and sales management tools that help developers reach potential buyers and improve conversion.
  • Complementary factors: Bank Indonesia reported a 25.67% year-on-year decline in primary residential property sales in Q1 2026, followed by a smaller 2.36% decline in Q2 2026, highlighting the need for stronger buyer engagement and sales conversion. Growing digital property searches, housing finance support and increasing use of online property platforms create further opportunities for PropTech solutions.

Qualification & Fitness

  • Technology Readiness: Startups with functional prototypes, pilot-ready or commercially available solutions in property technology, digital marketing, buyer analytics, immersive property experiences and customer engagement.
  • NUS Linkage: Open to NUS students, graduates, researchers, principal investigators (PIs), professors and startups supported through NUS Enterprise programmes, including BLOCK71, PIER71, TIG and National GRIP. Local and international startups with relevant solutions are also welcome, including those across BLOCK71's global network.
  • Commitment to Follow Through: Selected startups should be prepared to participate in business discussions, explore potential property applications and travel to Indonesia for further engagement or pilot activities where required.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia.
  • Timeline: November 2026 – April 2027.
