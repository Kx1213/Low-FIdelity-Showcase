Recover Value from Warehouse Dead Stock

Thematic Area: Market Access and Business Growth

How might we connect suitable warehouse dead stock with relevant buyers to recover value and release storage capacity?

Background

Recovering value from warehouse dead stock requires identifying goods eligible for sale, choosing suitable sales channels and coordinating the clearance process.

The objective is to turn authorised inventory into recoverable value while considering condition, pricing and fulfilment costs. Relevant partner capabilities include liquidation channels, wholesale marketplaces, multichannel commerce software and business-to-business sales tools.

Significance of the Problem

  • Continued storage pressure: Unsold stock occupies space needed for other warehouse activities.
  • Lower value recovery: Deteriorating condition and changing commercial relevance reduce the value of ageing goods.
  • Slower sales conversion: Fragmented listings and buyer follow-up leave suitable stock without a clear route to sale.

Potential Market Size

  • Broader goods-recovery activity: US retail returns were projected at US$849.9 billion in 2025, illustrating the scale of goods requiring disposition. This provides adjacent market context for inventory recovery and resale.
  • Established resale activity: Liquidity Services, a global surplus marketplace operator, reported US$1.57 billion in transaction value in FY2025, up 15%. This demonstrates resale activity through one operator.

Qualification & Fitness

  • NUS founders and programme support: Have an NUS student or graduate founder, or have received incubation support through an NUS programme such as B71, PIER71, TIG or the National GRIP Programme.
  • NUS researchers and academics: NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs) and professors.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to startups from Indonesia and other countries.
  • BLOCK71 network: Startups from BLOCK71 centres in Singapore, the United States, Japan, China, Vietnam and Indonesia.
  • Commitment: Be ready to complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia
  • Timeline: November 2026–April 2027.
