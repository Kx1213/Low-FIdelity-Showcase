Find Profitable Opportunities to Grow Delivery Revenue

Thematic Area: Logistic & Shipping

How might we identify underserved delivery needs and turn them into profitable growth in customers, orders and revenue?

Background

Growing delivery revenue while maintaining operational efficiency requires identifying customer groups, service areas and unmet delivery needs with the strongest commercial potential.

The objective is to identify a specific commercial opportunity and test its profitability. Approaches include analysing demand patterns, comparing service coverage with customer locations and assessing additional services.

Define the priority customer segment, sales process, available data and target outcomes during project scoping.

Significance of the Problem

  • Missed revenue: Unidentified customers and delivery needs limit sales opportunities.
  • Inefficient expansion: Investment in locations with insufficient demand and unattractive delivery economics reduces returns.
  • Weaker commercial decisions: Limited evidence makes it harder to prioritise services, customer groups and sales activity.

Potential Market Size

  • Global courier, express and parcel market: Mordor Intelligence estimates the market at US$725 billion in 2026, reaching US$963.9 billion by 2032.
  • Last-mile delivery market: Grand View Research estimates the global market at US$181.6 billion in 2026, growing to US$348.8 billion by 2033. This covers the final stage of delivery to customers.
  • Indonesian delivery market: Mordor Intelligence estimates Indonesia’s courier, express and parcel market at US$8.42 billion in 2026, reaching US$11.91 billion by 2031. These figures measure delivery services.

Qualification & Fitness

  • NUS founders and programme support: Have an NUS student or graduate founder, or have received incubation support through an NUS programme such as B71, PIER71, TIG or the National GRIP Programme.
  • NUS researchers and academics: NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs) and professors.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to startups from Indonesia and other countries.
  • BLOCK71 network: Startups from BLOCK71 centres in Singapore, the United States, Japan, China, Vietnam and Indonesia.
  • Commitment: Be ready to complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia
  • Timeline: November 2026–April 2027.

Market Validation

Find Profitable Opportunities to Grow Delivery Revenue

  • Strong demand from Indonesian merchants: Google, Temasek and Bain reported 2.6 billion video-commerce transactions in Indonesia in 2025, up 90%, with 800,000 sellers and online stores. This provides an active merchant base for delivery providers seeking new customers and services. The transaction count measures online purchases, rather than parcels delivered. (Source: Google (opens in a new tab))
  • Delivery growth is generating revenue: J&T’s Southeast Asian parcel volume reached 7.66 billion in 2025, up 67.8%, while regional revenue grew 39.8% to US$4.5 billion. The company expanded customers beyond major e-commerce platforms, demonstrating that broader customer coverage can support commercial growth. (Source: J&T Express (opens in a new tab))
  • Better delivery visibility helps win customers: Onfleet reports that SameDay Delivery grew its ShopRite accounts from 27 to 35 stores within a year, while monthly deliveries rose from around 8,000 to nearly 10,000. Delivery tracking and clearer capacity information helped the courier win back stores and serve more orders. (Source: Onfleet (opens in a new tab))
