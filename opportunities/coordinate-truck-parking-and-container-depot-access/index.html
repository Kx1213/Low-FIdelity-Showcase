Coordinate Truck Parking and Container Depot Access

Thematic Area: Logistic & Shipping

Problem Statement: How might we coordinate truck parking and container gate-in and gate-out movements to improve visibility and operating flow at a container depot?

Background

Container-depot operations require better coordination of truck parking and container entry and exit to improve traffic flow and site visibility.

The objective is to establish a clear view of arrivals, parking availability and gate movements so staff can organise depot activity. Relevant partner capabilities include site-mapping, movement tracking, access management and simulation.

Design the solution around the depot’s actual layout, operating rules and data sources. Document current gate and parking processes during project scoping.

Significance of the Problem

  • More difficult arrival planning: Disconnected arrival, parking and gate information complicates coordination with available capacity.
  • Slower response to congestion: Limited movement visibility delays identification of queues and bottlenecks.
  • Incomplete visit records: Disconnected parking and gate information complicates tracking of truck and container movements.

Potential Market Size

  • Indonesian logistics-facility context: Indonesia’s third-party warehousing market is estimated at US$3.57 billion in 2026, reaching US$5.51 billion by 2031. This provides adjacent industry context for facility-access and arrival-management tools rather than a depot-software market estimate.
  • Substantial container activity: Pelindo handled approximately 19.79 million TEUs in 2025, up 5.2%, providing a substantial operating base for truck-arrival, gate and container-movement coordination. A specific software opportunity requires eligible-site counts and annual software, integration and support budgets.

  • NUS founders and programme support: Have an NUS student or graduate founder, or have received incubation support through an NUS programme such as B71, PIER71, TIG or the National GRIP Programme.
  • NUS researchers and academics: NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs) and professors.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to startups from Indonesia and other countries.
  • BLOCK71 network: Startups from BLOCK71 centres in Singapore, the United States, Japan, China, Vietnam and Indonesia.
  • Commitment: Be ready to complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia
  • Timeline: November 2026–April 2027.
