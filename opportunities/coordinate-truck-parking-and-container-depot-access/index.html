Coordinate Truck Parking and Container Depot Access

Thematic Area: Logistic & Shipping

How might we coordinate truck parking and container gate-in and gate-out movements to improve visibility and operating flow at a container depot?

Background

Container-depot operations require better coordination of truck parking and container entry and exit to improve traffic flow and site visibility.

The objective is to establish a clear view of arrivals, parking availability and gate movements so staff can organise depot activity. Relevant partner capabilities include site-mapping, movement tracking, access management and simulation.

Design the solution around the depot’s actual layout, operating rules and data sources. Document current gate and parking processes during project scoping.

Significance of the Problem

  • More difficult arrival planning: Disconnected arrival, parking and gate information complicates coordination with available capacity.
  • Slower response to congestion: Limited movement visibility delays identification of queues and bottlenecks.
  • Incomplete visit records: Disconnected parking and gate information complicates tracking of truck and container movements.

Potential Market Size

  • Global yard-management market: ABI Research projects yard-management-system revenue at US$980 million in 2026, rising to US$4.9 billion by 2032. This covers technology for scheduling, gate automation and movement control across logistics facilities.
  • Parking-management technology: MarketsandMarkets estimates this broader market at US$7.22 billion in 2025, reaching US$12.41 billion by 2030. The category includes access control, reservations and parking operations across commercial, industrial and transport facilities.
  • Indonesian container activity: Pelindo Group handled 19.79 million twenty-foot-equivalent units (TEUs) in 2025, up 5.2% from 2024. This operating volume provides a substantial setting for truck-arrival, gate and container-movement coordination.
