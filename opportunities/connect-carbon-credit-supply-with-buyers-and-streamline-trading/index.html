Connect Carbon-Credit Supply with Buyers and Streamline Trading

Thematic Area: Climate and Clean Energy

How might we connect eligible carbon-credit supply with suitable buyers and manage purchases, sales and transfers more efficiently?

Background

Building carbon-credit buying, selling and intermediation capabilities requires access to suitable counterparties, experienced trading partners and a consistent process for progressing opportunities into completed transactions.

The objective is to build a repeatable commercial process covering buyer qualification, credit-offer comparison, quotations, contracts and transaction tracking. Relevant startup capabilities include carbon marketplaces, business-to-business sales software, transaction workflows and secure document sharing.

Significance of the Problem

  • Missed commercial opportunities: Disconnected buyers and credit suppliers limit transaction opportunities.
  • Slower transactions: Repeated document requests, unclear responsibilities and fragmented approvals delay agreements.
  • Greater execution risk: Incomplete records of credit status, ownership and transfer responsibilities complicate transaction execution.

Potential Market Size

  • Global voluntary transactions: Survey respondents reported transactions of 84.4 million tCO₂e worth US$535.1 million in 2024. Value fell 29% from 2023, showing an active but selective market. These figures cover voluntary transactions and exclude regulated allowance markets.
  • Indonesian exchange activity: Indonesia’s carbon exchange recorded 166,476 tCO₂e traded for IDR6.97 billion during January–September 2026, with 160 participants at September’s end. These figures measure activity on the exchange.

Qualification & Fitness

  • NUS founders and programme support: Have an NUS student or graduate founder, or have received incubation support through an NUS programme such as B71, PIER71, TIG or the National GRIP Programme.
  • NUS researchers and academics: NUS researchers, principal investigators (PIs) and professors.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to startups from Indonesia and other countries.
  • BLOCK71 network: Startups from BLOCK71 centres in Singapore, the United States, Japan, China, Vietnam and Indonesia.
  • Commitment: Be ready to complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.

Location & Timeline

  • Location: Indonesia
  • Timeline: November 2026–April 2027.
