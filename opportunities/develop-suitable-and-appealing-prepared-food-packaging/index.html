Develop Suitable and Appealing Prepared-Food Packaging

Thematic Area: Food Packaging Innovation & Product Presentation

How might we improve prepared-food packaging presentation and assess alternative materials that fit the product, handling and preparation conditions?

Background

An Indonesian healthy-food retailer operates ready-to-eat, ready-to-heat and ready-to-cook lines. The opportunity is to improve packaging materials, format and presentation for these products.

The objective is to develop a pack that presents the food clearly, supports convenient customer use and performs through its intended storage and distribution conditions.

Relevant startups offer packaging materials, structural design, printing, sealing and manufacturing services. The collaboration connects pack design with the selected product, filling method, storage conditions and preparation requirements.

Significance of Problem

The key challenges are:

  • Weak product presentation: Unclear packaging information makes purchasing and preparation more difficult for customers.
  • Packaging incompatibility: Unsuitable materials and seals create problems during filling, handling, storage and preparation.
  • Higher packaging costs: Material changes affect unit cost, production compatibility and handling effort across the product lifecycle.

Existing Efforts

  • Available material capability: A packaging-material developer produces biodegradable films using agricultural residues and publishes material specifications.
  • First application: Select one product and create packaging samples. Compare appearance, information clarity, handling, seal quality and unit cost with the existing pack under intended storage and use conditions.
  • Collaboration focus: Combine material supply, packaging design and conversion expertise. Assess food-contact documentation, barrier properties, sealing compatibility and heating or freezing requirements, with product-specific shelf-life testing as a separate workstream.

Potential Market Size

  • Global food-packaging market: The global food packaging market was valued at US$421.6 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach US$599.0 billion by 2033, with 4.3% annual growth during 2026–2033. Asia Pacific represented 40.0% of 2025 revenue. These figures cover all food-packaging materials and formats.

Eligibility / Qualification

  • Technology readiness: Minimum technology readiness level 3 (TRL3).
  • NUS linkage: NUS student or graduate founder, or incubation through an NUS programme such as BLOCK71.
  • Local and overseas startups: Open to Indonesian and overseas startups.
  • Commitment: Complete the selection process and travel to Indonesia if selected.
